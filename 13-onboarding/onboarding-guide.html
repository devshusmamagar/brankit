Onboarding Guide

SHUSMA Brandkit · Mobile App Onboarding Screens & Interaction Patterns

4-Step Onboarding Flow

Each screen pairs a full-bleed illustration with a heading, description, and action button. The user progresses through Welcome → Discover → Personalize → Shop with smooth slide or fade transitions.

Step 1 of 4

Welcome

Introduce the SHUSMA brand with a warm, luxurious hero image. Set the tone for a premium experience.

Step 2 of 4

Discover

Highlight curated collections and categories. Showcase the breadth of luxury offerings available.

Step 3 of 4

Personalize

Invite users to set preferences — style, size, interests — for a tailored shopping experience.

Step 4 of 4

Shop

Final CTA screen — transition from onboarding into the main app with a bold “Get Started” action.

Dot Indicator States

Three visual states communicate progress through the onboarding carousel:

StateAppearanceSpecUsage
InactiveSmall circle, light gold fill8×8px · #E2CC8A · radius 50%Steps not yet reached
ActiveElongated pill, gold fill24×8px · #C9A961 · radius 4pxCurrent step
CompletedSmall circle, dark gold fill8×8px · #A07D3F · radius 50%Steps already viewed

All transitions between dot states use transition: all 300ms ease.

Dark Mode

The onboarding fully supports dark mode. The background shifts to #1A1A1A, text to #FAF8F5, and accent colors remain gold-family for consistency. Illustrations should include both light and dark variants — supply onboard-*-dark.png assets.
