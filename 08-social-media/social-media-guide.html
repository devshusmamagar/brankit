Visual Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts for brand consistency

✓ Do

✓

Use cream (#FAF8F5) or dark (#1A1A1A) backgrounds consistently across all platforms

✓

Maintain generous white space — luxury breathes through minimalism

✓

Use gold (#C9A961) as accent color for borders, typography, and decorative elements

✓

Photograph products in natural golden hour lighting for warm, inviting tones

✓

Include the SHUSMA wordmark or logo on all branded visual content

✓

Use Playfair Display or similar serif fonts for all headline text overlays

✓

Keep a consistent filter/preset across all photography for visual cohesion

✗ Don't

✗

Never use neon, fluorescent, or overly saturated colors that clash with the gold palette

✗

Avoid cluttered compositions — less is always more in luxury branding

✗

Don't use casual or playful fonts (no comic sans, bubbles, or handwritten styles)

✗

Never apply heavy filters, excessive contrast, or unnatural color grading

✗

Don't use low-resolution images or pixelated graphics on any platform

✗

Avoid stock photos that look generic — invest in original, brand-aligned imagery

✗

Never place text on busy backgrounds without a semi-transparent overlay

Photography & Filter Guide

Maintaining a cohesive visual identity

☀ Lighting

  • Golden hour lighting preferred (first/last hour of sunlight)
  • Warm, soft directional light from 45° angle
  • Avoid harsh midday shadows and direct flash
  • Studio: use warm-white bulbs (3200K–4000K)
  • Subtle rim lighting to accentuate fabric textures

🎨 Color Grading

  • Warm white balance: 5200K–5800K camera setting
  • Lift shadows slightly to maintain creaminess
  • Boost warm tones: +5–10 on orange/red channels
  • Desaturate blues and greens by 10–15%
  • Add subtle vignette for focus and depth

📐 Composition

  • Rule of thirds with generous negative space
  • Center-aligned product shots for e-commerce
  • Symmetry and balance for flat-lay arrangements
  • Leave clear zones for text overlay (top/bottom 20%)
  • Use leading lines from gold decorative elements

📸 Technical Settings

  • Minimum 3000×3000px for all source photography
  • Shoot in RAW format for maximum editing flexibility
  • Aperture f/2.8–f/4.0 for portraits, f/5.6–f/8 for products
  • ISO 100–400 for clean, noise-free images
  • Export at 85% JPG quality for social media upload
