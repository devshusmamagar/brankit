01

Packaging System Overview

The SHUSMA packaging system delivers a luxurious, cohesive unboxing experience reflecting the brand's heritage and craftsmanship. Every touchpoint — from shipping box to tissue paper — reinforces premium positioning and creates a memorable brand moment.

Shopping BagRope handles · Matte lamination · Gold foil logo
Gift BoxCream stock · Gold emboss · Satin ribbon
Shipping BoxBranded tape · Tissue · Thank you card
Stationery SetFoil cards · Logo seals · Care labels

📦 Packaging Hierarchy

  • Outer Shipping: Corrugated mailer with branded tape — first brand impression upon delivery
  • Inner Box: Rigid gift box for in-store purchases and premium orders
  • Shopping Bag: Premium paper bag for retail carry-out, double-walled construction
  • Tissue Paper: Printed damask pattern, wraps garment inside all packaging
  • Inserts: Thank you card, care card, branded stickers, return label
  • Garment Bag: Breathable non-woven for high-value pieces, gold branding

✦ Design Principles

  • Restraint: Minimal external branding — let materials speak. Logo on one face only
  • Cohesion: Every element uses the same color palette, typeface, and logo mark
  • Tactile Quality: Papers, ribbons, and finishes chosen for premium hand-feel
  • Unboxing Journey: Sequential reveal — outer, inner, tissue, product, card
  • Cultural Respect: Arabic brand name شوسما appears with equal prominence
  • Sustainability: FSC-certified materials, soy-based inks, recyclable components

Brand Placement Guidelines

Logo placement ensures brand recognition while maintaining elegance and restraint. The golden ratio (1:1.618) guides positioning of all brand elements on packaging surfaces.

Shopping Bag — Front Face

  • SHUSMA logo centered, positioned at upper-third of bag face
  • Logo width: 40% of bag width (min 80mm on large bag)
  • Arabic شوسما beneath English logo, 8px gap, 60% of English size
  • Clear space: minimum 25mm on all sides of logo group
  • Gold foil stamp only — no printed ink on front face
  • Back face: small logo mark bottom-center, 20mm height

Gift Box — Lid Surface

  • Logo centered on lid, gold foil stamped or embossed
  • Logo width: 35% of lid width
  • Ornamental border frame: 8mm inset from edge, gold foil
  • Corner ornaments: small gold geometric accents at 4 corners
  • Interior lid: flocked velvet, no print
  • Base interior: SHUSMA label bottom-center, debossed

Shipping Box — Exterior

  • Logo stamp on one broad face only, centered, single-color brown
  • Logo height: 30mm (flexo print, no foil)
  • Arabic name beneath, 10mm height
  • Branded tape: repeating gold pattern with logo every 200mm
  • Tape applied in H-pattern (one lengthwise + two crosswise strips)
  • No printing on flaps or interior

Tissue Paper — Pattern

  • Gold damask pattern (vector repeat tile, 150mm × 150mm)
  • Pattern scale: 20% opacity on cream background
  • Logo integrated subtly into repeat — once per 2 repeats
  • Sheet size: 500 × 700mm, oriented for diagonal fold wrapping
  • Print method: flexo 1-color gold on 20gsm tissue

Environmental & Sustainability Notes

SHUSMA is committed to minimizing environmental impact while maintaining premium quality. All packaging decisions balance luxury presentation with ecological responsibility.

🌿 Material Sourcing

  • FSC Certification: All paper and cardboard must carry FSC Chain of Custody certification
  • Recycled Content: Minimum 30% post-consumer recycled content in corrugated shipping boxes
  • Soy-Based Inks: All flexo and offset printing uses soy-based or vegetable-based inks
  • Water-Based Coatings: Aqueous coatings preferred over UV lamination where possible
  • Cotton Rope: Handle rope from certified organic cotton suppliers
  • Vinyl Reduction: Transitioning stickers to paper-based alternatives by 2026

♻ Waste & End-of-Life

  • Recyclability: Shopping bags, gift boxes, and tissue paper are fully curbside recyclable
  • Compostable: Tissue paper and air pillows are home-compostable
  • No Mixed Materials: Avoid combining non-recyclable elements (foil + lamination on same layer)
  • Right-Sizing: Offer 3 box sizes to minimize void fill and shipping volume
  • Reusable Bags: Shopping bag weight and handle quality designed for customer reuse
  • Supplier Audit: Annual sustainability audit of all packaging vendors
Sustainability Goal: Achieve 100% recyclable or compostable packaging by Q4 2026. Eliminate single-use plastic from all packaging by Q2 2026. Current packaging is 85% recyclable by weight.
